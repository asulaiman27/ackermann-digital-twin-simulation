# Imported vehicle assets

These three GLB files are from the Quaternius Cars Bundle, distributed under
CC0 1.0 / public-domain terms:

- Sports Car A: https://poly.pizza/m/1mkmFkAz5v
- Sports Car B: https://poly.pizza/m/OyqKvX9xNh
- Performance SUV: https://poly.pizza/m/xsMtZhBkxL

The meshes are visual assets only. Vehicle mass, dimensions, tire parameters,
forces, and six-DOF motion remain controlled by the simulation profiles in
`app.js` and the Python dynamics model.
